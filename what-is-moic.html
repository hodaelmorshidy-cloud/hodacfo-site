What Is MoIC (Multiple on Invested Capital)?

Multiple on Invested Capital is the simplest private-equity return measure: everything you got back, divided by everything you put in. A 2.5x MoIC means $2.50 returned per dollar invested.

MoIC = total value (realized + unrealized) ÷ invested capital.

Illustrative: $100 invested returning $250 is a 2.0x–2.5x range outcome.

Why it matters

IRR can be gamed with timing — early dividends, subscription lines. MoIC cannot: it ignores time entirely. Reading MoIC and IRR together is how a MoIC of 2.0x achieved in 18 months is told apart from the same 2.0x over 8 years.

The practitioner trap

Quoting MoIC without the holding period. The pair is the answer: an LBO model should print MoIC and IRR side by side from the same equity cash-flow line, with the cash sweep driving both.
