Appraising One Project Is Easy

A discount rate and a row of cash flows will do it. The job you actually have is deciding which seven of twenty get funded, putting a defensible figure on the thirteen you refused, and then defending both lists to people who will push back. Free NPV templates answer the easy question — they do not build your cost of capital, they do not vary the hurdle by how risky the project is, and they have no concept of a budget. They rank nothing, allocate nothing, and cannot tell you what saying no costs.

In the shipped example, ranking by size would spend a third of the $4.5M envelope on the Cogeneration Plant before considering anything else. Ranking by NPV per unit of capital instead funds the DC Cooling Retrofit first — it returns 1.99 per dollar against the plant's 1.75 — and the budget, not the project list, is what decides who gets funded.

Common Questions

Can I break it?

Formula cells are locked so a stray keystroke cannot damage the model, and every blue input cell stays editable. There is no password — Review > Unprotect Sheet if you want to restructure something. If you do break it, run self_check.py from the toolkit and it will tell you which project stopped agreeing with an independent rebuild.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes, and that is tested rather than assumed. A build identical to this one in every formula and value was uploaded to Drive, opened as a Google Sheet, exported back to .xlsx and diffed against Excel cell by cell: all 26 tabs, 9 charts, 34 named ranges and the grouped engine rows came through, no error cells were introduced, and all 11,932 value-carrying formula cells matched Excel — including the crossover rate, the one cell most likely to degrade silently. Upload it to Drive and choose Open with ▸ Google Sheets, not Preview, which displays without converting.

I only have one project to appraise. Is this overkill?

Probably. Use one row of the register and you have a full single-project appraisal with a proper cost of capital — but the portfolio machinery is most of what you are paying for, and it only earns its keep when projects compete for a budget.

How do I know the numbers are right?

Every formula was recomputed in a live Excel engine with zero error cells. During development, 6,203 of them were independently rebuilt from raw drivers in a separate Python engine, zero mismatches.
