Solar Project Finance Model — Methodology & Benchmark

DSCR sculpting with the gearing cap that keeps the file non-circular, reserves and coverage ratios, the tax engine, the LP/GP waterfall conventions, and the verification record — published from the shipped Methodology PDF.

This page publishes the audit trail for the Solar Project Finance Model — an institutional-grade, 25-year utility-scale solar underwriting model: DSCR-sculpted senior debt, a construction period with capitalised interest, a funded DSRA, LLCR/PLCR, covenant testing, an LP/GP promote waterfall, a depreciation/NOL tax engine, a post-PPA merchant tail, probabilistic P50–P99 yield, and dated IRRs — plus five optional US-tax and structure modules, all shipped switched off. Pre-loaded with an illustrative 100 MW worked deal (not a real project); every default input is sourced and editable.

Debt Sizing — DSCR Sculpting, No Circular Reference

Target debt service(t) = CFADS(t) / TargetDSCR
Debt = PV(Target debt service, at interest rate)
Debt drawn = MIN(Sculpted debt, MaxGearing × Hard Capex)  ·  Scale = Drawn / Sculpted

Each period’s target debt service tracks the project’s own cash flow, so realized DSCR holds at (or above) the lender’s target for every period, and the loan provably amortises to zero by the end of the tenor. The gearing cap applies to hard capex, deliberately not total capex: sizing off hard capex breaks the debt → IDC → capex → gearing-cap → debt loop once construction interest is capitalised, and CFADS never depends on the debt, tax or IDC lines — so there is no circular reference and no iterative calculation anywhere in the file. On the worked deal the sculpted $135.0M exceeds the 75% cap; debt is capped at $120.75M, every period’s service scales down by the same factor, and realized DSCR holds at 1.56x against a 1.40x target — more conservative, never less.

Construction, Reserves & Coverage

Hard capex draws evenly over the construction months; senior debt accrues interest that is capitalised into total capex and funded by equity as it accrues (at the shipped default of 0 months, IDC = 0). The DSRA holds a set number of months of forward-looking debt service, funded at COD from equity and released as the debt amortises — it re-times equity cash, lowering equity IRR slightly while leaving total cash, project IRR, NPV and LCOE unchanged, and it revolves exactly to zero. Alongside period DSCR, the model reports LLCR and PLCR (PV of CFADS over remaining loan / project life at the cost of debt, over debt outstanding); min LLCR 1.56x, min PLCR 1.88x on the worked deal, with PLCR ≥ LLCR every period as the longer tail can only add coverage.

Tax Engine and Revenue

Depreciation is selectable — straight-line (default), declining-balance with an automatic switch, a custom schedule, or US MACRS at a selectable recovery class (IRS Publication 946 Table A-1, half-year convention). An optional ITC computes the credit on eligible basis, applies the statutory 50% basis reduction, and either uses or transfers the credit; an optional NOL carryforward pools losses (levered and unlevered tracked separately). Defaults reproduce the certified base case exactly. Revenue uses the contracted PPA price through the PPA term, then an escalating merchant curve for the remaining life — a real solar risk, since the offtake contract usually expires before the panels do.

Returns, Waterfall and Risk

LCOE (nominal) = (CapEx + PV(Opex)) / PV(Generation)
real rate = (1 + discount)/(1 + inflation) − 1
Equity IRR = XIRR(equity cash flows, actual calendar dates from COD)

The LP/GP waterfall runs preferred return → return of capital → GP catch-up → promote split, with two conventions stated plainly: the return-of-capital tier returns LP capital only, and the catch-up accrues against cumulative preferred paid, not total profit — both recognised market conventions, both exactly what the formulas implement, and the split conserves cash to machine precision every period. On the worked deal the LP earns the higher IRR (14.9% vs 13.1%) and the lower multiple (4.78x vs 8.51x) — correct behaviour for a back-ended promote over 25 years, flagged on the tab. From the P90/P50 ratio the Yield tab fits a lognormal distribution and reads exact exceedance factors at P50–P99 (cross-checked in development against a 2-million-draw Monte Carlo, agreement 1.5e-4). Covenant testing traps distributions below a lock-up DSCR and flags an event of default after consecutive breaches.

Verification Record

Every one of the 13,951 formula-string cells is independently recomputed in a clean engine and matched to the file’s stored values (tolerance |Δ| > 0.01 absolute or > 1e-6 relative). Each cash-flow module additionally has a from-scratch Python rebuild that reproduces its outputs to the cent and confirms it is neutral at defaults and correct when toggled. A degenerate closed-form golden case was verified in real Microsoft Excel to 9 significant figures; a structural audit confirms sources & uses balance to $0.00 and the debt amortises to a $0 ending balance.
