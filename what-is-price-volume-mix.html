What Is Price-Volume-Mix (PVM) Analysis?

Price-volume-mix analysis decomposes a revenue variance into three causes: what you charged (price), how much you sold (volume), and what you sold (mix). It is the difference between reporting a variance and explaining one.

Bridge form: price effect + volume effect + mix effect = total revenue variance — with the interaction term allocated, not dropped.

Illustrative bridge: a −4% revenue variance explained, not just reported. (Negative bars drawn by magnitude.)

Why it matters

A board asking “why is revenue down 4%?” will not accept “it just is.” PVM answers with causes that map to decisions: pricing, salesforce, product portfolio.

The practitioner trap

The two-way shortcut. Most free templates split price×volume only, silently burying the interaction effect inside whichever term computed last — the bridge then fails to tie to the actual variance. A correct three-way bridge carries a check cell whose only job is to prove the tie.
