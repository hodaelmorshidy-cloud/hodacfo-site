Refunds & Fixes

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Every product sold here is an instant digital download. That means it is sold on a fix-first basis rather than a returns basis: if something is genuinely wrong with a file, I correct it — I don't ask you to live with it, and I don't leave you without a remedy.

The short version. Something broken? I fix it, free, and send you the corrected file. Changed your mind about a file you have already downloaded in full? That is not refundable. If a defect turns out to be genuinely unfixable, you get your money back.

If something is wrong with the file

Email Hoda.elmorshidy@gmail.com with your order number and a screenshot of the problem. I will correct it and send you the updated workbook, normally within 2 business days, at no charge, for 12 months from your purchase date.

Things that count as a defect:

  • The file will not open, or opens corrupted.
  • A formula returns an error (#REF!, #VALUE!, #DIV/0! and similar) in the file as delivered.
  • A tab, feature, or calculation described on the product page is missing from the file.
  • A stated calculation is wrong — it does not do what the product page and the methodology documentation say it does.

If I cannot fix a defect in that list, you get a full refund. That is the backstop, and it is deliberate: a fix-first policy is only fair if there is a remedy when the fix fails.

What is not a defect

Because the scope of every product is published in full before you buy — each product page carries an explicit “is for” and “is not for” list, and the workbooks document their own limits — the following are not defects and are not refundable:

  • The model does not cover something listed as out of scope on the product page.
  • The outputs are not what you expected because of the assumptions you entered. Every model here calculates from your inputs; it does not validate them.
  • You bought the wrong product, or changed your mind after downloading.
  • You want a feature the product never claimed to have.

If you are unsure whether a product fits your situation, email me before you buy. I would rather answer that question than process a dispute afterwards.

Change-of-mind refunds

These are not offered. The file is delivered complete and immediately on purchase, and there is no way to return a downloaded spreadsheet. This is why the scope, the tab list, the function list and the worked example are all published on the product page before checkout — so the decision can be made with the facts in hand.

Your statutory rights

Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot be excluded under the consumer-protection law of your own country of residence. Where such rights apply, they apply in addition to everything above, not instead of it.

Each of those platforms operates its own refund and dispute process, and those terms apply to your purchase alongside this policy. If a platform's policy gives you more than this page does, the platform's policy governs.
