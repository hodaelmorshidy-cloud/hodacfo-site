Annual Budget & Reforecast Model — Methodology

Driver-based revenue, a one-switch scenario engine, the rolling latest estimate, and a price/mix/volume split that sums exactly back to the revenue variance — published from the shipped Methodology PDF.

By Hoda Elmorshidy • Fractional CFO • Financial Modeling • Corporate Finance since 2017 • Published 2026-08-17

This page publishes the methodology behind the Annual Budget & Reforecast Model — a driver-based operating model, not a budget grid. Revenue is planned from units and price rather than typed as a total; one scenario switch flexes the whole plan; actuals replace budget month by month; and every revenue variance decomposes into price, mix and volume the way an FP&A team does it. Built to be defended to a board, a lender, or a client.

Driver-Based Revenue and the Scenario Engine

Line Revenue = (Units × Active Volume Factor) × (Price × Active Price Factor)
Active Factor = INDEX(Factor Column, Scenario Select)
Monthly Expense Total = (Revenue × COGS % + Direct Costs) × Active Cost Factor

Scenario is a single input (1 = Base, 2 = Upside, 3 = Downside) selecting a row of volume / price / cost-inflation factors that feeds every tab. COGS is budgeted as a percentage of revenue so it flexes with volume automatically.

The Rolling Reforecast (Latest Estimate)

Latest Estimate = IF(Month <= Current Month, Actual, Budget)

Months up to the Current month pull actuals; later months keep budget. A closed month left blank reads as zero and pulls the estimate down — the formula behaving correctly, not a fault. The reverse matters too: actuals keyed beyond the Current month misstate every year-to-date figure until you advance it — costs entered ahead understate EBITDA, revenue entered ahead overstates it. The Actuals tab raises a REVIEW flag whenever data exists past the Current month, on either side; the latest estimate itself phases correctly either way.

Price / Volume / Mix — the Split That Reproduces

Price = (Actual Price − Budget Price) × Actual Units
Mix = (Actual Mix % − Budget Mix %) × Total Actual Units × Budget Price
Volume = (Total Actual Units − Total Budget Units) × Budget Mix % × Budget Price
Price + Mix + Volume = LE − FY Budget  (exactly)

Basis, stated plainly: Budget Price and Budget Units are the scenario-flexed values, phased to the months closed so far. The split is valued at budgeted price — the turnover method — because this tab bridges revenue. CIMA and ACCA value sales-mix and quantity variances at standard contribution when bridging profit, a different question; only a turnover-basis split sums back to the revenue variance, and the tab is labelled accordingly.

Sensitivity, Cash and Departments

The sensitivity grid flexes EBITDA across volume and cost-inflation; its 1.00x/1.00x cell equals the Annual Summary budget EBITDA for whichever scenario is selected — check it if you ever doubt the grid. The Cash Bridge converts accrual to cash with a single-month DSO/DPO lag capped at 30 days — a stated working-capital approximation, not a treasury forecast; the reconciliation closes by construction:

EBITDA (accrual) − FY Net Cash Flow = Δ Receivables − Δ Payables

Departments allocates each budgeted cost across cost centres; every allocation row must total 100% and the Check column flags any that does not with REVIEW — a warning, never a silent correction, because only you know which cell was meant to change.
