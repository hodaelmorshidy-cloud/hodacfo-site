What Is DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)?

Days Sales Outstanding measures how long revenue sits in accounts receivable before turning into cash. Thirty days of DSO means a month of your revenue is permanently loaned, interest-free, to your clients.

DSO = (accounts receivable ÷ revenue) × days in period.

Illustrative client comparison — the point is the spread, not the specific days.

Why it matters

Profit is an opinion; cash arrives on DSO’s schedule. For an agency, DSO by client is a profitability metric in disguise — a client who pays in 75 days costs margin no rate card shows.

The practitioner trap

Mixing periods: computing DSO with annual revenue but a month-end receivables snapshot, or hardcoding 365 into a quarterly model — invisible at the default, four times wrong at a quarter. Period length belongs in a labeled cell.
