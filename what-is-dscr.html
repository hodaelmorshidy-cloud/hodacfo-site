What Is DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)?

The Debt Service Coverage Ratio is cash flow available for debt service (CFADS) divided by debt service (principal + interest), measured per period. It is the number a project-finance lender actually tests — every period, not on average.

DSCR = CFADS ÷ (principal + interest), per period.

Why it matters

Debt sizing runs off it: sculpt the debt so each period’s service fits a target DSCR, and the covenant path is born. One weak period breaches, no matter how strong the average looks.

The practitioner trap

Reporting the average and hiding the minimum. On the Solar Project Finance Model’s worked deal, debt is sculpted to a 1.40x target and the shipped minimum is 1.56x against a 1.20x covenant — the minimum is the number that matters, and the model prints it, not the mean.
