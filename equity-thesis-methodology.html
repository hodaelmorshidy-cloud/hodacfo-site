Pre-Fill Sources — Stated Definitions, Not Just Numbers

Two definitional choices are disclosed up front. Capex uses the 10-K cash-flow line “Purchases of property and equipment and intangible assets” — the broad figure sell-side analysts use for FCFF. Net working capital is estimated as (AR + Inventory) − (AP + Accrued Liabilities) — a stated simplification; the 10-K provides the components if you need precision.

Why the Reverse DCF Is the Point

A conventional DCF asks “what is this company worth, given my assumptions?” The Reverse DCF inverts the question: given the market price, what growth is the market assuming? That turns a valuation argument into a falsifiable claim — you can judge whether the implied revenue CAGR is achievable rather than defending your own forecast. The Margin of Safety tab then converts fair value into maximum entry prices at fixed discounts, and the Holding Period Simulator prices Bear/Base/Bull exits on an EV/EBITDA multiple over 1–5 years, reporting IRR by year — so the thesis carries its own downside case.
